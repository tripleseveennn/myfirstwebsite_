Why TripleSeven?

There is a reason behind the three 7s.
Here is the backstory behind my online identity.

The Name & The 6th Grade Idea

“Tripleseven” comes from my name, which has 7 letters in each part—Glyziel Veronne Ranario. I came up with it back in 6th grade because I wanted a unique, niche username that felt like my own.

Since then, I’ve stuck with it and used “Tripleseven” as my username across all my social media accounts and even as my in-game name on the games I play. It basically became my little online identity, and honestly, I’ve just kept it ever since.

More Than Just A Number

777 is also often associated with luck, prosperity, and endless possibilities. In many cultures, the number 7 is considered sacred and is commonly associated with completeness and perfection.

So while “Tripleseven” started as a random username I made up in 6th grade, it eventually became something that feels a little more meaningful to me. It served as a small symbol of luck, growth, and the endless possibilities in store for me ahead.
